## Testing Notes

**Variables to experiment with in Leonardo:**
- **Models:** Phoenix, Leonardo Diffusion XL, SDXL
- **Guidance scale:** Try 7-12 range
- **Aspect ratios:** 4:3 (social), 1:1 (Instagram), 16:9 (Twitter/X header)
- **Negative prompts:** "text errors, misspelled words, blurry, low quality, watermark"
- **Key insight:** Leonardo handles composition/mood well but struggles with exact text — plan to overlay text programmatically in the app after generation

**Recommended workflow:**
1. Generate the visual/artistic background with Leonardo (no text in prompt)
2. Overlay ranking text, titles, and branding programmatically via canvas/SVG in the app
3. This gives pixel-perfect typography while keeping the AI-generated artistic flair
